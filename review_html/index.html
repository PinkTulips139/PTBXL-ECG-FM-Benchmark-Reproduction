Known limitations
  • Two of 78 physical canonical sample bundles are unavailable and appear as provenance-only entries.

  • Reviewer shards are serialized inspection data, not independent scientific results.
  • No fixed threshold defines “correct” or “incorrect” predictions here.
  • Larger paper-versus-reproduction differences are not assigned unproven causal explanations.
